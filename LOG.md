- 2026-10-21 Added FID/KID from the already-saved samples (`scripts/add_fid.py`), which overturned one conclusion and sharpened another: CelebA *does* get the window-width win (FID 128.3 -> 79.4) though its IS is flat, and EBMLS barrier 1.0, which beat the LS baseline on IS, is worse than it on FID. Split-multiplier control: x2 on half the schedule gives +1.22 (low-noise) or +0.28 (high-noise) IS against +3.51 for both halves — strongly super-additive, so the schedule must be widened coherently.
- 2026-10-22 Schedule *shape* beats schedule width: ramping the window multiplier down from 3.4 at t=0 to 1.6 at t=1 beats uniform x2 at matched memorization on CIFAR-10 (FID 83.3/86.1 vs 94.0/93.0 over two seeds, NN-RMS 0.267/0.271 vs 0.270/0.274, copies ~0) and on CelebA (FID 70.0 vs 79.4 at NN-RMS 0.301 vs 0.302, zero copies); neutral on MNIST. The up-ramp control fails, so the tilt direction matters and runs against the coarse-to-fine intuition. `--scale_multiplier_hi` in `scripts/mls_inception.py`.
