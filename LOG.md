- 2026-10-21 Swept the entropy barrier on CIFAR-10/MNIST/CelebA against an IS + nearest-neighbour-memorization eval (`scripts/mls_inception.py`). Negative result: EBMLS is dominated by plain LS with wider windows at every matched memorization level, because layer 2 as specified replaces rather than refines layer 1's estimate.
- 2026-10-21 Positive result from the control: uniformly scaling the per-step LS windows x2 takes CIFAR-10 IS from 2.78 to 6.29 with zero training-set copies, reproduced across two seeds; x2.5 and x3 gain more only by copying 15% and 51% of samples. Notebook `notebooks/mls_entropy_barrier_results.ipynb`, slides `results/mls_inception/2026-10-21_mls_slides.pdf`.
- 2026-10-21 Added FID/KID from the already-saved samples (`scripts/add_fid.py`), which overturned one conclusion and sharpened another: CelebA *does* get the window-width win (FID 128.3 -> 79.4) though its IS is flat, and EBMLS barrier 1.0, which beat the LS baseline on IS, is worse than it on FID. Split-multiplier control: x2 on half the schedule gives +1.22 (low-noise) or +0.28 (high-noise) IS against +3.51 for both halves — strongly super-additive, so the schedule must be widened coherently.
- 2026-10-21 Schedule *shape* beats schedule width: ramping the window multiplier down from 3.4 at t=0 to 1.6 at t=1 beats uniform x2 at matched memorization on CIFAR-10 (FID 83.3/86.1 vs 94.0/93.0 over two seeds, NN-RMS 0.267/0.271 vs 0.270/0.274, copies ~0) and on CelebA (FID 70.0 vs 79.4 at NN-RMS 0.301 vs 0.302, zero copies); neutral on MNIST. The up-ramp control fails, so the tilt direction matters and runs against the coarse-to-fine intuition. But its limit fails too: a flat schedule (`--flat_k`, same window at every step) sits *on* the uniform frontier, not the ramp's — IS 5.38 at NN-RMS 0.268 vs the ramp's 6.44 at 0.267 — so the gain is a partial reshaping, not flattening. `--scale_multiplier_hi` / `--flat_k` in `scripts/mls_inception.py`.
- 2026-10-21 Compressing the window schedule's dynamic range (`--scale_gamma`: base windows raised to a power gamma about their geometric mean, gamma=1 == uniform x2, gamma=0 == flat) is a second, FID-only win on CIFAR-10: FID 101.8/103.6/105.3/110.1 for gamma 0.75/0.60/0.45/0.25 at m=2, NN-RMS 0.288-0.291, zero copies, against ~121 for the uniform curve at the same distance -- ~430 FID per unit NN-RMS vs uniform's ~1400. IS says the opposite (5.17 -> 4.94) and I recorded the family as negative off IS before the FID landed; third IS/FID disagreement in this sweep. The flat control's FID also landed and is worse than uniform at the zero-copy point (k=17: 99.0 at 0.268 vs 94.0), not on its frontier as the IS numbers had suggested.
- 2026-10-21 Negative: neither end of the schedule is a free knob. At fixed peak multiplier (m1=1.6), raising m0 from 3.4 to 4.5 to 6.0 drives NN-RMS 0.267 -> 0.235 -> 0.163 and copies 0.2% -> 5.4% -> 31%, so low-noise width memorizes too; and at matched memorization both single-ended pushes (FID 73.4 at 0.235, 73.9 at 0.238) lose to the balanced ramp 4.2->1.8 (71.0 at 0.236). Ramp 3.4->1.6 is still the zero-copy operating point. Also fixed a reporting bug: both builders keyed schedule families off `method`+`scale_multiplier`, so flat (mult 1.0) and gamma (mult 2.0) runs were plotted and tabulated as plain uniform runs, one of them standing in for the x1 baseline.

- 2026-10-21 (level, compression) is confirmed as the parameterization of the window schedule, but it buys nothing new. Holding the level at the champion ramp's (m=2.5, kgeo 18.8 vs 18.5) and sweeping compression, gamma 0.90/0.75/0.60/0.45 gives CIFAR-10 FID 73.0/80.4/85.5/88.1 at NN-RMS 0.231/0.248/0.262/0.275 with copies 5.8%/1.2%/0.4%/0.2%. gamma=0.60 reproduces ramp 3.4->1.6's schedule to within one pixel at both ends (k0 11, kmax 31, kgeo 18.9 vs 11/31/18.5) and reproduces its metrics (85.5 at 0.262 vs 83.3 at 0.267), so the hand-tuned ramp was a point in this family all along. But the gamma curve at fixed level traces the same FID-vs-NN-RMS frontier as the down-ramp family rather than a better one -- marginally below it in the middle, above it at the de-memorized end -- and nothing here displaces ramp 3.4->1.6 (FID 83.3) as the zero-copy operating point. The transferable result is the gap between shape classes, not the class: compressed and tilted schedules sit ~7-10 FID below uniform and flat at every matched training-set distance.
