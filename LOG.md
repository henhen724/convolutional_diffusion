- 2026-11-02 Answered the RHM sampling-complexity question for the LS machine, and the answer is negative. Under hshunt's joint criterion (a generated grid counts only if it is NOT in the training set AND fully grammatical) there is no finite P: grammaticality and novelty are produced by the same mechanism, copying a training grid, so they never hold together. Where grammaticality is Theta(1) the cost is P* = v m^(Theta(n/(s^2-1))) = exp(Theta(n)) in the number of sites n = s^(2L), i.e. the same as memorizing the distribution -- the LS machine extracts no benefit from the hierarchy. Two legs. (1) Ceiling, derived and checked by enumeration: a k x k window reaches k//2 so it polices aligned blocks only up to side s^j*, j* = floor(log_s((k+1)/2)), capping grammaticality at C(k) = |R_L|/|R_j*|^(s^(2(L-j*))) = f0^U with f0 = m/v^(s^2-1), U = (s^(2(L-j*))-1)/(s^2-1), independent of P; over the 150 enumerable L=2 cases the closed form is exact in exactly the 70 drawn without a word collision and wrong in exactly the 80 with one (up in 48, down in 32 -- collisions renormalize f0, not U). (2) Cost, measured: filling the k-table is coupon-collecting N_k distinct windows, N_3 polynomial (13 602 at v=4,m=3,L=3) but N_5, N_7 still growing 5.4x and 8.9x per decade of P at P=3e5. Measured exponents at L=2,k=3 track the derivation: slope -3.19 in v (predicted -3), +1.57 in m (+1), and with f0 fixed succ_max falls 0.432 -> 0.0172 as U goes 1 -> 5 (predicted 0.375 -> 0.0074). Deck + notebook: notebooks/rhm_sampling_complexity_results.ipynb, results/rhm_sampling/2026-11-02/.

- 2026-11-03 Answered hshunt's straddling question, affirmatively and larger than they expected. New estimator (scripts/rhm_straddle_levels.py, job 50996974, 00:01:57, 160 cells): for each level j, tile the grid with *independent* uniform draws from R_j, the distinct leaf renderings of an aligned s^j block, so the grid is coherent to level j and scrambled above; a_j is the fraction of such grids whose every k x k window lies in the saturated pooled window set W. a_L = 1 is the saturation test; a_j < 1 for j > j* means the window rejects grids that are *already* coherent at level j, which only straddling windows can do. Result: at L=3, k=5 (j* = 1, so the window fully contains only 2x2 blocks) all 16 saturated grammars have a_2 < 0.99, median a_2 = 0.102 -- ~90% of level-2-coherent grids rejected, i.e. the window is policing the root two levels above j*. At L=3, k=3 it is 19/30 seeing two levels up, 4 one, 7 none, and v2m2 a_1 ranges 0.392-1.000 over five rule seeds, so reach is a property of the grammar and not of k. Root detection 1 - a_{L-1} falls monotonically in m (L=2 k=3: 0.771, 0.579, 0.379, 0.297, 0.108 for m=2..6; k=5 decays far slower, 0.798 -> 0.578) -- more synonyms means more legal boundary pairs.
